# Volkswagen Jetta (Mk6 Generation, 2011-2018)

## Overview
The sixth-generation Jetta was a full redesign with a larger interior and,
notably, a cost-reduced rear suspension (a simpler torsion-beam setup) on
base and mid trims in order to hit a lower starting price point -- a change
that drew some criticism from reviewers at launch relative to the outgoing
generation's more sophisticated setup. A mid-generation facelift arrived
around the 2015 model year, bringing revised styling, an updated
infotainment system, and available active-safety features on higher trims.

## Trims
Common trims: S, SE, SEL, SEL Premium (later years), GLI (sport), TDI
(diesel), and a Jetta Hybrid variant offered only for the 2013-2014 model
years.

## Engines and Drivetrain
- Early base engine: 2.0L naturally aspirated four-cylinder, roughly 115 hp.
- This was later replaced as the base engine by a 1.4L turbocharged
  four-cylinder, roughly 150 hp, offering better fuel economy.
- Mid-range trims commonly used a 1.8L turbocharged four-cylinder, roughly
  170 hp.
- GLI trim: 2.0L turbocharged four-cylinder, roughly 210 hp.
- TDI trim: 2.0L turbo-diesel four-cylinder, roughly 140 hp.
- Front-wheel drive only across the generation; no AWD variant offered.

## Fuel Economy
TDI models were rated up to roughly 45 mpg highway by EPA estimates. Gas
engine trims ranged from roughly the mid-20s to mid-30s mpg combined
depending on the specific engine and model year.

## Safety
Later model years (particularly post-2015 facelift) introduced available
forward-collision warning and adaptive cruise control on upper trims --
these were not standard or available across the whole generation, so
availability depends heavily on trim and model year.

## Important Note: Diesel Emissions Recall
TDI diesel models from this generation fall within the range of model years
affected by Volkswagen's 2015 diesel emissions violations. Many affected
vehicles were subject to manufacturer buyback or recall/software-update
programs. Anyone evaluating a used TDI from this generation should check
whether the specific vehicle's emissions recall was completed.

## Known Reliability Notes
Some owners of early 1.8T and 2.0T engines from this generation have
reported water pump and timing chain tensioner concerns. As with any
commonly-reported pattern, this reflects frequency of owner complaints, not
a confirmed defect rate -- worth a mechanic's inspection on a specific used
example rather than a blanket assumption.
